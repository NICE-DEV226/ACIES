## Logo Concept

The three concentric hexagons represent the three levels of perception:
1. **Outer** — low resolution (64p-128p), cheap, low information
2. **Middle** — medium resolution (224p-320p), balanced
3. **Inner** — high resolution (512p-1024p), expensive, high information

The solid core with white dot is the **decision point** — where enough
information has been gathered to make a confident decision.

The connecting lines represent the **adaptive flow** — the controller
navigates between perception levels based on cost-risk optimization.

The hexagonal shape (not circular) suggests **precision and structure**
over organic randomness.
